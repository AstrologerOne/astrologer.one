## 🧠 Our Approach

Astrologer.one is being developed with a focus on:

**Astronomy → Calculations → Astrology → Practical Tools**

Rather than relying entirely on third-party astrology services, the platform is designed around our **own calculation engine**, with support for multiple traditions and calculation methods.
